## What this service is

Heir of Wisdom is a practice tool. A child answers reasoning questions and then
works through an explanation of the pattern behind each answer, so the reasoning
is visible rather than only scored. A parent holds the account and is the person
the practice belongs to.

Practice here is exactly that: practice. It is not a test, it is not a
diagnostic report, and it produces nothing that is reported to anyone.

## What the practice is not

- It does not produce an official score on any assessment.
- Nothing in it predicts admission to a programme, a class, or a gifted
  placement. A practice answer says what a child chose on one question, and
  nothing about what any test would return.
- It is not published, licensed or endorsed by the organisations that publish
  CogAT, OLSAT and NNAT. Those names appear only to say which kind of reasoning
  is being practised.

## What this build does

The build in front of you runs in demo mode and has no accounts. There is no
sign-in, no parent dashboard and no child's record yet. Demo mode is not
protected: nothing in it is stored securely or kept apart from anyone else's,
and the app labels it as demo wherever it appears.

The same is true of the pages here. Nothing you type into them is stored, and
this build ships no analytics, advertising or tracking.
