## Before you read anything else

This notice has not been approved. No qualified legal reviewer has read it. It
describes what this build actually does, so that you can see exactly what is
involved — not what the service is intended to become. When a reviewer has read
it, this page will say what they found and what they changed.

## What exists today

The build in front of you has no accounts. There is no sign-in, no parent
dashboard and no child's record yet. Two things follow, and both matter more
than anything else on this page:

- Nothing you enter anywhere in this build is stored, and nothing about a child
  is held by it.
- The sections below describe what a child's record will hold once accounts
  exist. They are written from the behaviour of the practice, not from a finished
  account system.

## Who holds the account

An account belongs to a parent or guardian. A child never has an account, and
nothing here is addressed to a child directly.

## What a child's record holds

A child's record is set up by the parent who holds the account, and it holds:

- an age band chosen from a short list, never a date of birth
- the assessment goal chosen for that child
- an experience level
- a schedule preference
- interests, if the parent adds them
- the answers the child gives during practice

A practice session records which questions a child was shown and which answer
they chose. The explanations are text that ships with the app; they are not
generated from anything about your child.

## What a child's record does not hold

- No full legal name for a child.
- No date of birth. The age band is the whole of it.
- No school, address, photograph or contact detail for a child.

If any of that changes, this page changes with it.

## Demo mode

This build runs in demo mode. Demo mode uses sample data so the practice can be
tried without an account, and the app labels it as such wherever it appears.

Demo mode is not a protected version of the real thing. There is no
authentication in front of it, and nothing it shows is stored securely or kept
apart from anyone else's. Read every screen in demo mode as a demonstration of
how the practice works, not as a private record of your child.

## Analytics and tracking

No analytics, advertising or tracking script ships in this build, and no third
party receives anything a child enters.

## Reviewing and deleting a child's record

We intend to give a parent a way to read a child's record and to delete it. That
control does not exist yet — there is no account, no dashboard and no deletion
screen — so this page does not describe one or point you at it. When it exists,
this section will say where it is and what deleting it removes.
