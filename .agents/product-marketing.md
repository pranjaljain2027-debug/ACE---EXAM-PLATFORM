## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Heir of Wisdom is a premium visual reasoning and cognitive-learning platform that helps children discover spatial and logical rules through hands-on manipulation—guiding children to explore patterns, uncover rules, and experience the joy of independent discovery.

**What it does:** An interactive visual reasoning studio for children ages 5–13. Every question comes to life through visualizations, explanations, and interaction. Children explore patterns, relationships, and the thinking behind an answer across CogAT, OLSAT, and NNAT-style reasoning domains (Verbal, Quantitative, Nonverbal; figure analogies, matrices, paper folding, classification, sequences, shape patterns, verbal analogies). Parents set goals; the platform designs the learning journey; the child experiences learning. A parent dashboard shows concise interpretation, evidence, and next steps—not IQ-like scores or percentiles.

**Product category:** Educational technology / cognitive development / gifted assessment preparation (entry points and milestones, not the entire product)

**Product type:** SaaS web application (Next.js 16 + React 19)

**Business model:** Freemium trial → paid subscription (parents sign up, try, pay)

## Target Audience

## Core Growth Opportunity

**Children learn to isolate visual attributes and apply patterns to new contexts; parents gain visibility into cognitive development.**

**How Heir of Wisdom supports this growth:**
- Interactive canvas where kids physically rotate, reflect, scale, and translate shapes (Tactile Manipulation)
- Five-stage pedagogical engine: Observe → Experiment → Discover → Apply → Transfer
- Far-transfer challenges with completely different geometric objects to prove conceptual generalization
- Guided 3-tier scaffolding so children never feel helpless or judged
- Calm, beautiful architectural environment fostering quiet focus (no gamified distraction)
- Parent dashboard with granular data on hint dependency, practice accuracy, and transfer strength
- Mastery requires recognition, explanation/demonstration, application, and transfer to a materially different visual form

**What it unlocks:** Confidence in visual reasoning, excitement about independent problem-solving, time spent in calm focused engagement, pride in the child's growing abilities

**Emotional arc:** Proud of the child's raw ability → Excited about the child's ability to think independently → Confident that school performance translates to real-world thinking

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators (stated positively, without contrasting others):**
- Interactive canvas where kids physically rotate, reflect, scale, and translate shapes (Tactile Manipulation)
- Five-stage pedagogical engine: Observe → Experiment → Discover → Apply → Transfer
- Far-transfer challenges with completely different geometric objects
- Guided 3-tier scaffolding so children never feel helpless or judged
- Calm, beautiful architectural environment fostering quiet focus
- Parent dashboard with granular data on hint dependency, accuracy, and transfer strength
- Mastery requires recognition, explanation/demonstration, application, and transfer

**How we do it differently:** The child experiences learning through a reasoning loop: Question → Think → Answer → Understand → Explore/manipulate → Transfer → Discovery/recognition.

**Why that's better:** Proves true conceptual mastery; builds confidence through hypothesis testing.

**Why families choose us:** They want their child to see what others miss, not just pick the right bubble.
