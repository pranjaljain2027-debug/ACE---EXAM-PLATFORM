- **Style keywords:** editorial, premium, architectural, warm, calm, matte materials, soft daylight, precise highlights, digital interface, screen-based, touch interface

- **Color palette:** Sand #FAF7F0, Navy #0E2235, Forest #1F4A34, Ochre #B57424, Terracotta #B84E26
