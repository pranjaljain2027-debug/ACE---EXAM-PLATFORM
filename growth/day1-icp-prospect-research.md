## ICP Definition (Final)

**Parents of children aged 5–13 who are currently spending $50–$200/month on cognitive enrichment** (workbooks, Kumon, tutoring, brain-training apps) **whose child is ready to explore new problems and develop reasoning skills.**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Child age** | 5–13 (sweet spot: 8–10) |
| **Decision-maker** | Parent (typically mother) |
| **Current spend** | $50–$200/month on cognitive enrichment |
| **Child's profile** | Aces school worksheets; ready to explore novel problems and develop reasoning skills |
| **Trigger event** | Teacher comment, gifted assessment result (CogAT/OLSAT/NNAT), or parent noticing child is ready to apply a learned pattern to a new format |
| **Emotional state** | Proud of ability; excited that school smarts = real thinking; optimistic about enrichment that builds confidence |
| **Alternatives considered** | More worksheets, more tutoring hours, another subscription app |

## Where to Find ICP Prospects (Day 1 Action List)

### Priority 1: Reddit — r/giftedchildren & r/homeschool

**Search these terms:**
- `site:reddit.com/r/giftedchildren "spatial reasoning" "my child"`
- `site:reddit.com/r/homeschool "visual reasoning" "workbook"`
- `site:reddit.com/r/giftedchildren "pattern" "my child"`
- `site:reddit.com/r/homeschool "problem" "new" "my child"`

**What to capture per prospect:**
- Reddit username
- Child's age (if mentioned)
- Enrichment context (workbooks, tutoring, apps)
- Specific curiosity or exploration quoted (verbatim)
- Link to comment/thread

### Priority 2: Facebook Groups (Gifted/Talented Parent Networks)

**Search for groups:**
- "Gifted and Talented Parents [Your State]"
- "Homeschool Enrichment [Your City/State]"
- "CogAT Prep Parents"
- "Visual Reasoning for Kids"

**What to capture per prospect:**
- Parent name (first name only)
- Child's age/grade
- Enrichment products mentioned
- Contact channel (FB messenger, email if listed)

### Priority 3: GreatSchools.org / Nextdoor Education Threads

**Search:**
- "gifted assessment prep [your city]"
- "CogAT prep [your city]"
- "spatial reasoning tutor [your city]"

**What to capture per prospect:**
- Parent name
- Location
- Enrichment goals stated
- Contact method (Nextdoor message, etc.)
