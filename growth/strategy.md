## Two Customer Questions (Verbatim)

1. "Will this help my child for real?"
2. "How long will it be before I see some improvement in my kid?"

**Implication:** The dominant objection is not price or awareness — it is **efficacy skepticism** (Q1) and **time-to-value anxiety** (Q2). Parents have likely been burned by brain-training apps or drill games that promise results but don't transfer. Any channel we choose must answer proof-of-results and speed-of-results first.

## Two Candidate Channels

### Channel A: Community Engagement (Reddit + Facebook)
**Tactic:** Answer cognitive-development questions in r/giftedchildren and relevant Facebook groups with short, non-spammy responses that include a 15-second screen recording of a child solving a rotation puzzle on hierofwisdom.me, then link to the free trial.

- **Addresses Q1 ("Will this help for real?")** with visual proof of a kid manipulating shapes, not just claims.
- **Addresses Q2 ("How long?")** by showing a concrete "Stage 1 → Stage 2" progression in the demo.
- **[Evidence]** The repo already has a `ReasoningCanvas` teaser on the Landing Page and the `HowItWorksPage` with 5 stages — we have demo assets ready.
- **[Guess]** We don't know if Reddit/Facebook users will click through, or if mods will ban promotional links.

### Channel B: Email Re-engagement of Trial Users
**Tactic:** Export the list of parents who signed up for hierofwisdom.me but did not pay. Send a 3-email sequence: (1) acknowledge the skepticism ("Will this help for real?"), (2) show a specific timeline of what improvement looks like week 1 vs week 4 ("How long?"), (3) limited-time offer to return.

- **Addresses both questions directly** in the customer's own words.
- **[Evidence]** You confirmed parents "sign-up try and pay us," which implies a signup funnel exists with trial users who didn't convert.
- **[Guess]** We don't know the size of that list, whether you have email permissions, or what the historical trial-to-paid rate is.
